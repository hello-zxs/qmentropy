I am Zheng Xiangsheng, a physics graduate of Xiamen University Malaysia, now pursuing an MSc at Western University in London, Ontario. My work circles one question: how geometry shapes the propagation of light — transformation optics, geodesic lenses, and quantum optics at the single-photon level.

This site is my public workbench: derivations, ray-tracing simulations, lab records, and the indexes I build while reading. I try to keep everything reproducible — assumptions in the equations, parameters in the code, diagrams for the experiments.
