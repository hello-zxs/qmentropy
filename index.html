03 / The paper

The thesis, in full.

The anchor of the lab: a BSc thesis on the Morse lens, its equivalent geodesic surfaces, and the ray tracing that checks them.

BSc (Hons) Thesis · Xiamen University Malaysia · 2026

Ray tracing and equivalent geodesic lenses for the Morse lens

We construct the equivalent geodesic-lens representation of the Morse refractive-index profile, show that the conformal Morse lens is the (A = B, C = 1) subclass of Cornbleet's general family, and validate the construction by ray tracing.

05 / About the lab

A notebook, not a monument.

QMENTROPY is the research side of a three-site matrix. The Index is the living signal; the Record is the formal dossier; this is where the work is kept.

I am Zheng Xiangsheng, a physics MSc student at Western University. The lab collects the computations behind the writing: ray tracers, conformal maps, surface constructions, and the occasional figure that took longer than the paragraph it illustrates.

The rule is simple: if a figure appears in a note, the model and the parameters should be available. If a claim appears in a paper, the assumption that could break it should be visible. The Lab is where that standard is enforced.
