From the Morse profile to a ray从 Morse 剖面到光线通解

This research note extends the web thesis in scalar geometrical optics. The surface-of-revolution construction below also works for any differentiable positive radial index on each regular interval. For the Morse potential, retaining its energy offset makes the ray equation explicitly solvable. The GIF sweeps shape at fixed energy and impact; the controls also let you vary the latter two.

这份研究笔记延伸了原论文的标量几何光学模型。下面的旋转曲面构造适用于任意正的、可微的径向折射率。对于 Morse 势，保留能量偏移后仍能显式求出光线。动图固定能量与入射参数，只扫描形状；滑块可分别调整三者。

Write the shifted Morse potential as \(V=D(x^2-2x)\), with \(D>0\). In the logarithmic coordinate of the conformal map, a translation sets the radial scale \(R\), the depth sets the positive index scale \(N\), and \(\epsilon=E/D\) is the dimensionless energy. The map contributes the factor \(R/r\) to the radial index. With \(R,N>0\) and any real exponent \(k\), define

将 Morse 势写成 \(V=D(x^2-2x)\)，其中 \(D>0\)。在共形映射的对数坐标中，平移决定半径尺度 \(R\)，势阱深度决定正折射率尺度 \(N\)，能量偏移为 \(\epsilon=E/D\)。映回径向平面后，折射率多出因子 \(R/r\)。取 \(R,N>0\)，指数 \(k\) 可为任意实数：

\[x=(r/R)^k,\quad h(x)=\epsilon+2x-x^2,\quad n(r)=N\,\frac{R}{r}\sqrt{h(x)},\quad \rho=nr=NR\sqrt h.\]

Use only \(x>0,\ h>0\); a propagating interval requires \(\epsilon>-1\). The original thesis is \(R=N=1,\ \epsilon=0,\ k=1/B=-a>0\) on \(0<r\le R\). The formulas also apply beyond the waist \(x=1\) and to negative \(k\), which exchanges the inner and outer physical radii. The interactive view displays the inward branch \(k=1/B>0,\ 0<x\le1\).

传播域满足 \(x>0,\ h>0\)，存在这样的区间要求 \(\epsilon>-1\)。原论文对应 \(R=N=1,\ \epsilon=0,\ k=1/B=-a>0,\ 0<r\le R\)。公式也覆盖 \(x=1\) 以外的另一支；取负的 \(k\) 会互换内外物理半径。交互图显示 \(k=1/B>0,\ 0<x\le1\) 的内支。

Arbitrary initial conditions任意初始位置与方向

Conservation of \(L=nr\sin\psi\), where \(\psi\) is measured from the radial direction, gives \( (dr/d\phi)^2=r^2[(nr/L)^2-1]\). Set \(\ell=L/(NR)\), \(u=1/x\), \(w=k(\phi-\phi_0)/\ell\), and \(d=\ell^2-\epsilon\). For \(k\ell\ne0\) the first integral reduces to a constant-coefficient equation:

令 \(\psi\) 为光线与径向的夹角，守恒量 \(L=nr\sin\psi\) 给出 \( (dr/d\phi)^2=r^2[(nr/L)^2-1]\)。取 \(\ell=L/(NR)\)、\(u=1/x\)、\(w=k(\phi-\phi_0)/\ell\)、\(d=\ell^2-\epsilon\)。在 \(k\ell\ne0\) 时，一阶积分化为常系数方程：

\[u_w^2=-d u^2+2u-1,\qquad u_{ww}+du=1.\]

\[u(w)=u_0 C_d(w)+v_0 S_d(w)+T_d(w),\qquad r=R\,u^{-1/k}.\]

\(d=0\)\(1\)\(w\)\(w^2/2\)

At the launch point set \(u_0=(R/r_0)^k\) and \(v_0=-u_0\sqrt{h(x_0)}\cos\psi_0\), retaining the radial direction. This determines both branches and their turning points; use the solution only while \(u>0\). Changing the sign of \(L\) reverses the azimuth. A radial ray (\(L=0\)) stays on a meridian up to a pole or material boundary. A singular cone tip alone does not select a unique continuation. At \(k=0\), \(\rho=NR\sqrt{1+\epsilon}\) is constant: unroll the equivalent cylinder in \((\rho\log(r/R),\rho\phi)\) to get straight lines.

初值为 \(u_0=(R/r_0)^k\)、\(v_0=-u_0\sqrt{h(x_0)}\cos\psi_0\)，保留径向方向的符号。由此同时得到两支轨迹及转向点，只取 \(u>0\) 的物理部分。改变 \(L\) 的符号会反转方位角。\(L=0\) 的径向光线沿子午线到达极点或介质边界；奇异锥尖处的延拓需要额外条件。\(k=0\) 时 \(\rho=NR\sqrt{1+\epsilon}\) 为常数，等效面是圆柱；在展开坐标 \((\rho\log(r/R),\rho\phi)\) 中，光线为直线。

Which parts have a real surface?哪些区域存在真实曲面？

For any positive radial index, matching the optical and surface metrics forces \(\rho=nr,\ ds=n\,dr\) along an outward meridian. Thus up to an axial translation, reflection and admissible choices of slope sign, the exact surface height is a quadrature. On each regular interval a real Euclidean surface of revolution exists exactly where the radicand is nonnegative:

对于任意正的径向折射率，比较光学度规与旋转面度规，向外子午线上必有 \(\rho=nr,\ ds=n\,dr\)。由此得到高度的精确求积公式；轴向平移、镜像和允许的斜率符号给出不同放置方式。每个正则区间上，欧氏三维旋转面存在的充要条件是根号内非负：

\[z(r)=z_* \pm\int_{r_*}^r \sqrt{n(q)^2-[n(q)+q n'(q)]^2}\,dq,\quad \left|\frac{d\rho}{ds}\right|=\left|\frac{kx(1-x)}{h(x)}\right|\le 1\quad\text{(Morse)}.\]

\[\frac{z-z_*}{NR}=\pm\frac{1}{|k|}\int_{x_*}^{x} \frac{\sqrt{h(t)^2-k^2t^2(1-t)^2}}{t\sqrt{h(t)}}\,dt.\]

The geodesic is then \((\rho\cos\phi,\rho\sin\phi,z)\) on each admissible branch. At zero energy put \(x=1-\cos\theta\), so \(\rho/(NR)=\sin\theta\). On \(0\le\theta\le\pi/2\), the full sheet exists for \(B\ge1/2\). Below that threshold the pole is excluded; the real annulus starts at \(\cos\theta=B/(1-B)\). The apex half-angle above threshold is \(\arcsin(1/(2B))\). Continuing through the waist needs a separate embedding check on the outer branch.

每个可嵌入分支上的测地线为 \((\rho\cos\phi,\rho\sin\phi,z)\)。零能量时令 \(x=1-\cos\theta\)，则 \(\rho/(NR)=\sin\theta\)。在 \(0\le\theta\le\pi/2\) 的整个内支上，存在等效曲面的条件为 \(B\ge1/2\)。低于阈值时极点不可嵌入，真实圆环从 \(\cos\theta=B/(1-B)\) 开始；高于阈值时锥顶半角为 \(\arcsin(1/(2B))\)。若延伸到腰线另一侧，须重新检验嵌入条件。

\[\frac{s}{NR}=B(\theta+\sin\theta),\qquad \frac{|dz|}{NR}=\sqrt{B^2(1+\cos\theta)^2-\cos^2\theta}\,|d\theta|.\]

The wireframe shows only the connected real sheet reached from the rim. A positive energy offset can give an infinite axial end; the plotting window then stops at \(\sqrt x=0.07\) unless the embedding fails earlier. At most two azimuthal turns are drawn; an open ray is also clipped below \(r/R=0.012\). The height is integrated numerically; the ray uses the analytical solution. The moving dot advances in normalized optical length, using \(d\sigma/d\phi=\rho^2/L\). Each displayed segment takes six seconds to replay.

线框只显示从边缘连续可达的真实曲面。正能量偏移可能产生轴向无限长的端部，此时绘图截在 \(\sqrt x=0.07\)，若先遇到嵌入边界则提前终止。每条光线最多显示两圈，开放光线进入 \(r/R<0.012\) 后也会截断。高度按上式数值积分；光线按解析解计算。移动光点按归一化光程播放，使用 \(d\sigma/d\phi=\rho^2/L\)；每个显示区间的回放时间均为六秒。

Releasing the thesis constraint \(A=B,\ C=1\)解除原论文的 \(A=B,\ C=1\) 约束

There is a second way to generalize the thesis. In the three-parameter meridian family of Šarbort and Tyc, Eq. (29), choose \(C>0\), \(\rho=\sin\theta/C\), \(0<\theta<\pi\). The two sheets meet at \(\theta=\pi/2\). The following parametrization avoids solving an implicit equation for \(n(r)\):

原论文还可以沿另一条路径推广。对 Šarbort 与 Tyc 式 (29) 的三参数子午线族，取 \(C>0\)、\(\rho=\sin\theta/C\)、\(0<\theta<\pi\)，两支在 \(\theta=\pi/2\) 相接。下列参数形式可直接给出 \(r,n,z\)，无需先反解隐式折射率方程：

\[s=\frac{A\sin\theta+B\theta}{C},\qquad \frac{r}{r_*}=\left(\frac{\sin\theta}{\sin\theta_*}\right)^A \left(\frac{\tan(\theta/2)}{\tan(\theta_*/2)}\right)^B,\qquad n=\frac{\rho}{r}.\]

\[\left(\frac{dz}{d\theta}\right)^2=\frac{(A\cos\theta+B)^2-\cos^2\theta}{C^2}.\]

The radial formula follows by integrating \(d\log r=ds/\rho\); fix its scale with any regular reference point. Work on monotone branches, treating a zero of \(ds/d\theta\) or a pole separately. The height exists wherever the right-hand side is nonnegative. For a nonradial ray put \(m=C|L|\), \(0<m<1\) and \(Q=\sqrt{\sin^2\theta-m^2}\). Its azimuth on each monotone leg is \(\phi=\phi_* \pm [F(\theta)-F(\theta_*)]\), with

由 \(d\log r=ds/\rho\) 积分即得径向公式，任选一个正则参考点固定尺度。在各单调分支使用这些式子，极点或 \(ds/d\theta=0\) 处单独处理。高度公式右侧非负才存在真实旋转面。对非径向光线令 \(m=C|L|\)、\(0<m<1\)、\(Q=\sqrt{\sin^2\theta-m^2}\)。在每个单调轨迹段，方位角为 \(\phi=\phi_* \pm [F(\theta)-F(\theta_*)]\)，其中

\[F(\theta)=A\arccos\!\left(\frac{m}{\sin\theta}\right) +B\,\operatorname{atan2}\!\left(Q,m\cos\theta\right).\]

Differentiation gives \(F'=m(A\cos\theta+B)/(\sin\theta\,Q)\), the Clairaut ray equation. Switch legs at \(Q=0\), keeping azimuth continuous. At \(A=B,\ C=1,\ r(\pi/2)=1\), the radial map reduces to \(r=(1-\cos\theta)^B\), recovering the thesis. Select A, B, C above to vary these meridian parameters independently. At fixed impact fraction, C changes the optical scale of the entire surface while the normalized planar path stays the same. The scale bar shows this change.

求导得 \(F'=m(A\cos\theta+B)/(\sin\theta\,Q)\)，正是 Clairaut 光线方程。在 \(Q=0\) 的转向点连接下一段，并保持方位角连续。当 \(A=B,\ C=1,\ r(\pi/2)=1\) 时，径向映射化为 \(r=(1-\cos\theta)^B\)，还原原论文。选择上方的 A, B, C 模型可独立调整三个参数。固定入射比例时，C 改变整个曲面的光学尺度，归一化的平面轨迹相同；右图的比例尺显示这一变化。

At \(m=0\) use a meridian; at \(m=1\) a regular waist admits the circular geodesic. Negative \(C\) gives the same first-sheet meridian after changing the angle convention. The first-sheet \(C\to0\) limit at fixed \(\rho\) is \(s=(A+B)\rho\): a cone, or a plane at \(|A+B|=1\), provided \(|A+B|\ge1\).

\(m=0\) 时取子午线；\(m=1\) 时正则腰线容许圆形测地线。改变角度约定可吸收 \(C\) 的负号。固定 \(\rho\) 考察第一支 \(C\to0\) 的极限，得到 \(s=(A+B)\rho\)；当 \(|A+B|\ge1\) 时为圆锥，等号对应平面。

The first source defines the thesis convention, Chen gives the zero-energy conformal family, and Šarbort and Tyc describe the metric correspondence. The energy-offset solution here is a derivation from those starting assumptions. The check compares it to independent Cartesian Hamiltonian integration and tests the surface metric.

原论文给出本文采用的参数约定，Chen 给出了零能量共形族，Šarbort 与 Tyc 给出了度规对应。此处的能量偏移解由这些出发条件推导。验证程序独立积分笛卡尔坐标下的哈密顿方程，并检查曲面度规。

Across nine rim-launch tests, including either side of the critical case, the largest Cartesian discrepancy was \(1.66\times10^{-8}R\). The largest sampled relative meridian metric error was \(8.63\times10^{-6}\) or less. These are finite-grid checks. The suite also tests off-waist launches, negative exponents, the fish-eye hemisphere, and the overlap of the two models.

九组边缘入射测试覆盖了临界值及其两侧，笛卡尔坐标中最大的轨迹偏差为 \(1.66\times10^{-8}R\)；子午线度规采样的最大相对误差小于 \(8.63\times10^{-6}\)。这些数值来自有限网格。程序还检验了腰线以外的初始点、负指数、鱼眼半球，以及两个模型的重合情形。
